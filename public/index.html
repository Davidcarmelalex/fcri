THE INSTITUTE

A wider horizon.
A rigorous purpose.

Fusion Civilization Research Institute brings together questions about intelligence, governance, technology and the future of human society.

Our purpose is to turn ambitious ideas into research that can be examined, educational material that can be taught, and systems that can be tested. We welcome thoughtful collaboration across disciplinary and geographic boundaries.

FCRI’s direction is shaped by a simple commitment: knowledge should expand human possibility, and its claims should remain open to scrutiny.
