# FCRI — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in FCRI research and development a respectful, inclusive, and intellectually rigorous experience for everyone.

## Standards

### Research Integrity
- All research must be reproducible and well-documented
- Data sources must be cited and verifiable
- Methodologies must be transparent
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed

### Collaboration
- Respect diverse perspectives and expertise
- Provide constructive feedback
- Credit contributors appropriately
- Share knowledge openly

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Plagiarism or data fabrication
- Harassment or discrimination
- Intentional misrepresentation of findings
- Suppression of valid research

## Enforcement

Report violations to conduct@fcri.science. All reports investigated within 72 hours.
