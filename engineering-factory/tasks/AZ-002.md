# AZ-002: Review policy authorization before agent side effects

New proposed task; begin after AZ-001 is reviewed and integrated.

Evidence: AzraelOrchestrator._process calls agent.handle before authorizing
the returned response. Authorization therefore cannot prevent side effects
inside handle. PolicyEngine also authorizes every action for CRITICAL events.

Propose an explicit separation of planning, authorization, and execution.
Document the intended approval matrix and obtain the product owner's policy
decision before changing authorization semantics. Test that denied actions
never execute, approved actions execute only once, and errors are auditable.
Use mocks only, without live targets. Do not claim the current seven tests
establish safe execution behavior. Deliver a design and tests in a separate PR.
