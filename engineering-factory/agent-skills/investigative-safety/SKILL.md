# Investigative agent safety and evidence skill
## Allowed without coercive authority
Public-source research, citizen-provided evidence parsing, URL/domain metadata, transaction timeline extraction, sandbox malware analysis, hashes, custody logs and officer-ready findings.
## Human/legal gates
No access to subscriber KYC, bank accounts, private CDRs, interception, identity unmasking, freezes, search/seizure or statutory closure without specific lawful authorization and officer approval. Evidence metadata must reflect source and collection time; hashing does not imply judicial admissibility.
## Skill tests
- Correlate public fraud indicator across synthetic complaints without declaring suspect identity.
- Present source citations, timestamps, confidence, corrections, contradictions, integrity hashes and chain of custody.
- Critic rejects unauthorized enrichment and prevents execution even when the planner recommends it.
- Never expose victim information to the public visual demo.
