# MR NOTHING Open Source Manifesto

MR NOTHING exists as a tribute to friendship, loyalty, builders, dreamers, and the advancement of humanity through open technology.

We believe intelligent mobile systems should be transparent, extensible, and accessible.

Our mission is to create an open Android-native AI agent platform that empowers humans rather than controls them.

Core principles:
- openness
- accessibility
- transparency
- human empowerment
- developer freedom
- responsible innovation
