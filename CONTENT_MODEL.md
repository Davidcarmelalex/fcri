# FCRI Content Model

## Primary Surfaces

- Home
- Research archive
- Papers
- Academics
- Fellowships
- Executive programs
- Support / donations
- Partnerships
- Media

## Institutional Narrative

The site should communicate:

- frontier science seriousness
- coherent institutional identity
- research depth
- educational pathways
- credible support and donor alignment

## Content Standards

- avoid startup-style hype language
- prefer institutional clarity
- tie every major route back to research, education, or mission support
- keep route language consistent with a real institute, not a generic landing page
