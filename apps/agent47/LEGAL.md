# Licensing, attribution and brand rights

## Software
The parent FCRI repository has an MIT LICENSE (Copyright (c) 2026 David Carmel Alex / Voltex Network). This subproject currently follows that repository license. Preserve its copyright and license notices when redistributing covered software.

## Branding
Original AGENT 47 logos and artwork are included as project assets. MIT permission for software does **not** establish trademark registration or exclusivity. Before asserting exclusive brand rights or licensing the artwork separately, confirm the rights holder and obtain appropriate legal review. A brand-use policy cannot retroactively revoke rights already granted under the repository's existing MIT license.

## Third-party dependencies
FastAPI, Uvicorn, pytest, httpx and their transitive dependencies have their own licenses. Docker base images and GitHub Actions also have separate terms. Generate a pinned dependency lock/SBOM and verify all license notices before distributing a production build. Do not assume all packages use MIT.

## Deployment / compliance
No government license is generally required merely to run a private Ubuntu-hosted dashboard, but data protection, security testing authorization, export controls, and other legal obligations depend on jurisdiction and actual integrations. Do not market this foundation as certified or fully compliant.

## Before public launch
1. Conduct a trademark clearance search for AGENT 47 in relevant countries/classes.
2. Decide the legal owner of the brand and verify authority to license it.
3. Produce an SBOM, third-party notices and vulnerability scan from a reproducible build.
4. Publish privacy terms, retention policies, security contact and acceptable-use terms appropriate to real data processing.
5. Review third-party names and integration logos before use.
