# AZRAEL — Phase 1 Master Blueprint (1 November 2026)
**Demonstration:** 4 November 2026 | **Phase 1 acceptance milestone:** 24 November 2026
**Status:** Engineering requirements, not an assertion of completed deployments or official API access.

## Mission
AZRAEL combines sovereign cybercrime intelligence, safe multi-agent investigation, digital-evidence integrity, empathetic Indian-language citizen intake, and persistent follow-through. **Report once. Be heard. Stay informed. Never be forgotten.** No observable citizen contact should disappear silently; this is an operational objective, not a guarantee that every case can be solved.

## AZRAEL CARE: empathetic multilingual conversation
- Dedicated landline/mobile/SIP intake, official WhatsApp text/voice/media, and safe outbound communication when approved/provisioned.
- Disclose AI identity; calm, compassionate, nonjudgmental trauma-informed dialogue. Handle silence, panic, crying, interruption, code-switching and citizen correction, with immediate handover to a trained person where required.
- Target 22 scheduled Indian languages plus English with measured per-language recognition, translation and synthesis. Candidate stack: Sarvam STT/TTS/translation, IndicTrans2, approved local Indic LLM and optionally ElevenLabs, subject to language tests, data sovereignty and security.
- Adaptive interview: online financial fraud, UPI, impersonation, account takeover, harassment, extortion, stalking, malware and child endangerment. Ask minimally, preserve facts; never request OTP/password/PIN.
- For current financial loss, direct immediate action through official 1930 and bank reporting channels. Do not claim that conversation creates an FIR or guarantees recovery.
- Respect safe-channel contact preferences, explicit recording notice/consent as required and human assistance options.

## AZRAEL SENTINEL: contact recognition and zero-unanswered protocol
- Log all **observable** calls and messages, including delivered, answered, missed, abandoned, disconnected and retry events with timestamps and provider references.
- Caller ID is unverified contact metadata, NOT proof of a person; support shared, spoofed, withheld and reassigned numbers, provisional references and anonymous assistance.
- Cross-reference prior AZRAEL contact/case records only through verification and permitted access. Telecom subscriber KYC, location and CDRs require lawful specific authorization and telecom provider mechanisms; assume no general lookup API.
- Durable missed-contact recovery queue, safe callback permission, deduplication, outages/failover and supervisor escalation. Never call back when it could endanger the citizen.
- Measure acknowledgment latency, missed-call backlog, callback completion and human escalation.

## AZRAEL OMNIMIND: case continuity and lawful memory
- Distinct person, contact number, conversation, complaint, identity-assurance and evidence records with event sourcing; persist authorized recordings/transcripts, corrections, conversation summaries, multilingual preferences, evidence and officer actions.
- Returning callers should resume only after identity/case authorization. Do not require the victim to repeat an already documented traumatic account.
- Keep clear provenance: citizen allegation, raw artifact, transcription, AI hypothesis, verified finding and officer decision. Source-link every material claim.
- RBAC/ABAC, purpose controls, encryption, retention/holds, audit of reads, data corrections, safe deletion, abuse protections and limited cross-case correlations. No blanket indefinite surveillance dossier.

## Investigation orchestration
- Run case-scoped Planner DAGs with strict tool scopes and Critic veto for every sensitive action.
- Extract authorized financial transaction references, fraud URLs, communication identifiers and evidence metadata; analyze citizen-provided evidence, public OSINT, malware in sandbox, potential patterns, and digital forensic timelines.
- Generate SHA-256/SHA3 evidence integrity where applicable, chain-of-custody records, PoJ event references, and reviewed BSA section 63 draft certificates. Hashing does not by itself guarantee court admissibility.
- Prepare legal section hypotheses, intelligence graphs, correlation confidence and officer-reviewable next steps. Never assert suspected identity/guilt from shared phone numbers alone.
- Human review and proper authority are mandatory for sensitive personal-data disclosure, bank freezes, interception, searches, blocking, formal FIR registration, statutory orders and official investigation closure.
- AI may resolve defined non-statutory assistance requests automatically, but **cannot itself close police criminal investigations**.

## Case Guardian: proactive justice continuity
- Every accepted case has an owner, priority, next action, next citizen update, operational SLA, and escalation path.
- Verified status updates sent by the system over safe, consented WhatsApp/SMS/voice channels, including honest no-change notices where appropriate. Do not fake investigative progress.
- If the officer does not respond, internally remind and escalate to supervisors rather than placing the burden on the complainant. Retry failed updates with delivery tracking and safety controls; do not spam traumatized victims.
- Lifecycle: CONTACT_RECEIVED -> INTAKE -> TRIAGED -> REFERRED/ASSIGNED -> INVESTIGATING -> PENDING_EXTERNAL -> REVIEW_FOR_DISPOSITION -> CLOSED_BY_AUTHORITY. Also transferred, duplicate, withdrawn and reopened, with recorded reasons and role controls.
- Metrics: contact acknowledgment, time to triage, P0 escalations, evidence integrity, repeated storytelling burden, missed SLAs, citizen notifications, officer accountability and confirmed dispositions.

## Officer handover
- Show complaint summary, all authorized contextual conversations, verified identity assurance, event timeline, linked evidence/hashes, relevant financial facts, investigated leads, confidence and provenance, unresolved questions, statutory constraints, pending approvals and safe-contact preferences.
- Route to the responsible geographic/subject unit through verified jurisdiction policies, with default human-supervised assignment. Allow officer corrections and priority override with audit reason.
- Supervisor panel shows stalled cases, overdue queues, unexplained lost contact, workload, safe callbacks and resolution accountability.

## Priority
P0 immediate ongoing loss, life/child safety danger: prompt human escalation and official guidance. P1 active extortion/account takeover/serious threats: accelerated specialist review. P2 standard retrospective reports: structured review. P3 nonurgent advice/reporting: appropriate workflow. Reevaluate on new facts; never rank just by loss amount.

## Architecture
Citizen -> telecom/WhatsApp gateway -> speech/language router + empathetic CARE agent -> SENTINEL contact event registry -> OMNIMIND case memory -> priority engine -> Planner / isolated Critic -> authorized domain agents -> deterministic custody / PoJ -> officer review -> Case Guardian proactive updates -> citizen.

Separate public ingress from sovereign investigative enclaves with mTLS, least privilege, encrypted evidence, audited access, malware-scan ingress, restricted egress and statutory retention.
